About the studio

A beautiful home starts with a good plan.

We are space planners for Brooklyn homes. Before any furniture is bought or any wall is moved, we work out the layout: how big each room should be, how the rooms connect, how people move through the house, and exactly where everything goes.

We plan for how a frum family really lives. Two work zones in the kitchen. A dining room that seats everyone on Yom Tov and still feels right on a Tuesday. Bedrooms that fit a growing family. A clear path from the kitchen to the sukkah, and real storage for Pesach. Once the plan is right, we can help with finishes and styling too.

Services

Space planning, from the whole house to one room.

01

Full-Home Space Planning & Floor Plans

A complete layout for the whole house: room sizes, how the rooms connect, circulation, and a clear, measured floor plan for you and your contractor.

02

Room Sizing & Proportions

How big should the dining room be? How deep is the living room? We set dimensions and proportions so every room feels right and works.

03

Kosher Kitchen Layouts

Separate meat and dairy zones, each with its own sink, prep space and storage. We plan where the ovens, the dishwashers and a Pesach setup go.

04

Dining & Yom Tov Hosting Layouts

A dining room sized for the Yom Tov crowd, with space for extension leaves, walkways behind the chairs, and a route from the kitchen that works.

05

Renovation & Extension Planning

Before you renovate or build out, we plan the new layout and test the options. Then we work alongside your architect and contractor.

06

Furniture Layout & Storage

Furniture placed at true scale, bedrooms that fit big families, seforim walls, and storage for Pesach dishes and Sukkos decorations.

Also: finishes & styling

Once the layout is right, we can help choose finishes, furniture and colors that suit it. Not sure where to start? Tell us about your home.

Process

From measuring tape to final floor plan.

A clear, calm process so you always know what's next.

  1. 01

    Consultation & Measuring

    We visit, measure the house, and talk about how your family lives: how many at the table, who sleeps where, and what isn't working.

  2. 02

    Existing Plan

    We draw your home as it is today, to scale, so every decision starts from real dimensions.

  3. 03

    Layout Options

    Two or three layout options with room sizes, flow and furniture placement, and the trade-offs explained.

  4. 04

    Final Floor & Furniture Plan

    A measured floor plan and furniture plan, including the kosher kitchen layout and the dining room setup for Yom Tov.

  5. 05

    Build Support & Finishing

    We work with your architect and contractor while the plan is built, and help with finishes and styling if you'd like.
